Our Story

Founded in 2025 in CBD, Delicious Bakery started with one goal: to bring real, handcrafted bread to Cape Town.

Mission

To provide high-quality, affordable baked goods made with love and local ingredients.

Vision

To be Cape Town's go-to bakery for fresh daily bread and custom cakes.
